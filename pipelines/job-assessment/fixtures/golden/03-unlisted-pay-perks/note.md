# Senior Technical Writer, Ashgrove Example Software

This is an invented job posting used to test a job assessment tool. Ashgrove Example Software does not exist.

## About the role

We are a fast-paced team that builds a mobile app and the services behind it. You will work closely with product managers and other stakeholders.

## What you will do

- Write guides and reference pages for developers.
- Engineers are busy and answer questions when they can.
- We keep a short style guide in our wiki.
- You can document our mobile SDK for iOS and Android.

## What we are looking for

- At least 11 years of professional technical writing experience.
- Strong command of API reference writing and OpenAPI files.
- Comfortable with Kubernetes concepts because our product deploys on it.

## Pay and benefits

- Compensation is discussed with candidates who reach the second interview.
- Every employee gets an extra five paid days off each year, on top of vacation.
- Each person has a yearly learning budget for books, courses and conferences.
