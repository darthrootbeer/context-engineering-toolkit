# Technical Writer, Video Tutorials, Driftmark Example Media

This is an invented job posting used to test a job assessment tool. Driftmark Example Media does not exist.

## About the role

Driftmark sells training courses for a software product. We are a friendly team and we work from anywhere.

## What you will do

- Most of your week will be recording and editing screen-capture video tutorials.
- You will also coordinate our translation vendors and review their delivered files.
- Writers have a weekly session with the product engineers to check facts.
- You will pick the authoring tools together with the lead writer.
- Style is decided by the marketing team, and there is no written guide.

## What we are looking for

- 12+ years of technical writing experience.
- Fluent in DITA XML and a component content management system.
- Experience documenting iOS and Android SDKs.

## Pay

- Region A: $105,000 to $120,000.
- Everywhere else: $85,000 to $100,000.
